# AI adoption throughout the business

I was interested in AI progress and the business wanted to gain AI adoption to help deliver faster. They created an AI COP to help with this, but I heard big claims about where it would go and what it could do in the short term and the plans for doing it. I thought what was being said was possible but trying to follow the plan that was suggested would lead to a lot of frustration for development teams and we wouldn’t get much if any adoption.

<aside>
💡

> The suggested plan was that we would go from traditional SDLC models and practices, immediately to everyone being able to do everything and we would no longer need any 1 role as AI can do everything so should just embrace it

</aside>

I offered to do the COP with the idea that I can steer conversations on a more logical track and help gain adoption in a more sustainable way for everyone. The person who was leading the delivery of AI adoption went off on long term sick leave and I was asked to drive the initiative.

- I spoke at a town hall
- got volunteers who were interested in helping
- identified specific areas of the SDLC that AI can support/remove/augment
- considered which area give the most value for the least effort
- regularly spoke to people to ensure I knew what innovations were taking place
- senior were primarly focused on speed of delivery, I changed those conversations to be focused on quality *1
- I created agents and coached others on how to create them and how to think about how to use AI
- I worked with engineers, testers, PO’s, designers and leadership to discuss elements of their role and how they streamline it with AI
- I had pushback at times which sometimes meant leaving that area alone until later
- I had people vibe coding solutions thinking that they were now developers and they had the answers to bigger problems - I had to guide these people carefully. Their passion and ideas were appreciated but it could annoy some that can see why their solution would not work….ever e.g. Will North (Security director) providing a “Solution” where chat GPT explores the web site and writes tests with no domain knowledge

<aside>
💡

***1**

I made the change through multiple conversations with senior leadership and considered their view and understood why they spoke about it in that way.
I highlighted that none of our OKR’s mention speed of delivery but the do heavly focus on quality and while some form of delivery speed should be meaured, it should not be the metric we focus on as AI will give you that in the long run.
I spoke about how we are failing to meet best practices due to lack of time and that showed how this is degrading our software and delivery time (tech debt, no docs, bugs etc…) and that happens because people are so focused on fast delivery they dont get time to do the other stuff. Conversation on quality are the foundations of a good product that continues to grow

</aside>
