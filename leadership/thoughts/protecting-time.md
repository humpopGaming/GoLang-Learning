# Overview
Managing and protecting your time effectivly helps you break your tasks down better and gain satisfaction from the work you do. Andy Grove, who was one of the founders and long-term CEO of Intel broke all magerial work into 4 buckets:
- Information gathering
- Decision-making
- Nudging
- Being a role model

## Your time
- calendars
    - meeting with others
    - appointments with self
    - default visible to all for transparancy (set to private when needed)
    - use reminders to help remember
    - add itenary for each event (define what success for a given event)
    - include breaks to make sure you get sufficent down time
    > Your calendar is only for organizing your time. Nothing more.
- to do list
    - all required tasks that need doing go on here with a sufficent explination to know what they are for
- email
    - only check emails a specified times of the day, use its asyncrouns nature to your advantage
    - a zero inbox policy is nice depending on the number of message you get
    - only archive any communications incase you need to find them in the future
- creating agents for repetative tasks where possible

## Your teams time
- nothing that will risk the sprint
- consider that dev time needs them to be in flow state
- ask individuals what time meetings work best for them
