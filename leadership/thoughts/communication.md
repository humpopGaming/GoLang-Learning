# Overview
Communication can come into 3 buckets, spoken, written and non verbal. A manager should always be looking to improve their communication skills in the way a developer is trying to improve their coding skills because this is the corner stone of the managers job. From deligating tasks to communciating a vision, it is the managers role to ensure all necessary communication takes place across the team and that the message is delivered correctly

## Communication mediums
 - Face-to-face: Best for important, meaningful conversations requiring human connection and body language. Synchronous and hard to plan, but can lead to genuine, unscripted moments.
 - Email: Formal and asynchronous. Ideal for information you'll want to refer back to — great for paper trails and non-urgent communication.
 - Documents: Best for formalizing ideas and designs. Collaborative docs let multiple people contribute and refine content with full formatting capabilities.
 - Chat software: Great for quick, informal team communication. Feels synchronous but can move fast — not ideal for formal or complex discussions.
 - Use-case specific software such as ticket trackers or project-management software: Excellent for structured workflows like sprint planning, but limited in functionality compared to general communication tools.


## Communication requirements
All communication should be carefully considered with the rule of thumb being all comunication should be concise, clear, and trustworthy.
Communication is a two way street and any form of communication should be engaged in as such. Emails should be written for the consumer, face to face communicaiton should ensure that the both (all) parties are part of the conversation. do this by being genuinly interested in their input, leaving space for them to share their thoughts and activly inviting them to do. try and understand what brings them to their conclusion and what alternatives they see. 

Be aware of each individuals different requirements for communication, how do they like to recieve praise or guidence. Preferences vary wildly.
