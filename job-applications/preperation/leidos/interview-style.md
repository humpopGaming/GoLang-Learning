Leidos Engineering Manager interviews typically focus on behavioral and situational questions rather than deep technical coding, assessing leadership, conflict resolution, and team collaboration. 

Common Question Categories:

Behavioral & Fit: Questions often use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to evaluate past experiences.  Examples include "Tell me about a time you had to work under pressure," "How do you handle conflict with a coworker?", and "What is your biggest strength or weakness?"
Technical Leadership: Candidates are asked about their approach to Agile methodologies, managing resources, budgets, and timelines on complex projects, and ensuring code quality in large systems.
Strategic & Operational: Queries may involve resource management, balancing workloads, and identifying innovations to improve processes. Some roles may include case studies or technical challenges related to specific domains like cybersecurity, cloud computing, or data analysis. 
Cultural Alignment: Leidos values integrity, innovation, agility, collaboration, and dedication.  Interviewers assess how well candidates align with these core values and their ability to work in a team-oriented, defense-focused environment. 
Preparation Tips:

Review Your Resume: Be prepared to discuss every project and technology listed in detail. 
Highlight Soft Skills: Emphasize teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving abilities. 
Prepare for Technical Discussions: Be ready to discuss fundamental software engineering concepts, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), and cybersecurity principles relevant to the specific role. 
Ask Questions: Show genuine interest by asking about team dynamics, ongoing projects, and company culture.
