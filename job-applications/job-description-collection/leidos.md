The UK National Security, Defense and Intelligence Sector at Leidos currently has an opening for a Software Engineering Manager to work remotely. This is an exciting opportunity to use your experience supporting the Metropolitan Police contract.

In this role, you will lead software engineering teams responsible for the design, development, and quality of applications and services. You will provide technical leadership and people management, ensuring engineering standards, best practices, and system integrity are consistently maintained. This position focuses on engineering excellence, team development, and technical governance, working in close partnership with delivery leadership.

Your Role and Primary Responsibilities

    Lead and manage software engineering teams delivering complex applications and services

    Provide technical leadership and guidance, ensuring adherence to engineering best practices

    Line manage engineers including performance reviews, career development, and mentoring

    Ensure code quality, maintainability, scalability, and security

    Establish and enforce engineering standards and development practices

    Drive adoption of modern engineering approaches (CI/CD, automation, code reviews)

    Support engineers in solving complex technical challenges and removing blockers

    Own and manage Interface Control Documents (ICDs), including:

        Creation, review, and ongoing maintenance of ICDs

        Ensuring interface definitions are accurate and up to date

        Managing interface changes and dependencies

        Maintaining traceability between ICDs and system requirements

    Collaborate with architects and technical leads on system design

    Ensure technical documentation is produced and maintained

    Support governance, compliance, and audit requirements


Basic Qualifications

    Experience as a Software Engineering Manager or similar role

    Strong background in software development

    Experience managing engineering teams

    Experience delivering software across full lifecycle

    Understanding of system integration and interface management

    Experience maintaining technical documentation

    Strong stakeholder communication skills


Preferred Qualifications

    Experience in enterprise or government environments

    DevOps / CI-CD / Azure exposure

    Agile delivery experience

    Jira and Confluence experience

    Microservices and API design knowledge

    Experience with system integrations and third-party interfaces

    Understanding of security and governance requirements
